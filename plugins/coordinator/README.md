# Coordinator

Basic task and calendar coordination for a small office, on personal Claude (Free or Pro).

**Status: scaffold (0.0.1).** The folder layout, manifests, skill outlines, memory templates and eval layout exist. The skills are not written yet. Do not install this for real use.

What it will do: keep tasks, assignees (including other staff) and due dates in your own notes; read meetings from your Google Calendar; answer "today", "this week", "on Tuesday", "completed" and "what did Bob do"; produce a "needs you" list by hand, or on a schedule on Pro or higher. It will not send, post or pay without your yes, and it knows about other people's work only from what you log.

See `evals/EVAL-PLAN.md` for how it will be tested.
